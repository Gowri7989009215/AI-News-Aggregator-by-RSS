Verification Principles

How NewsHub Guarantees Data Integrity

Canonical Link Hygiene

All URLs undergo strict syntactic validation and parameter stripping (removing utm_*, at_*, fbclid, tracking tokens, and fragments) to produce clean canonical links.

Original Date Integrity

We never fabricate publication dates. Original publisher timestamps are preserved, distinguishing between when an event was published versus when it was fetched, and flagging outdated stories (> 48h).

Cross-Feed Deduplication

When multiple outlets cover the same wire story, our Jaccard headline similarity algorithm merges duplicate entries, elevates the primary Tier 1 publisher, and cross-references alternative sources.

Claim Disambiguation

Articles containing speculative or unconfirmed assertions are explicitly labeled as Developing / Unverified Claims. Breaking news status is strictly restricted to verified, recent reports.

The Verification & Validation Pipeline

Four automated stages applied to every ingested news item before presentation.

1. Syntactic Link Validation & Canonical Sanitization

Every incoming article link is audited with urllib.parse. Links lacking valid http/https protocols or containing malformed hostnames are rejected. Tracking parameters (e.g. utm_source, utm_campaign, at_medium, ocid) and URL hash fragments are stripped to produce a unique, invariant canonical identifier.

2. Authentic Timestamp Preservation

NewsHub parses true publisher publication dates via published_parsed and ISO dateutil parsing. If a feed omits a valid timestamp, the article is labeled "Publication date unverified" rather than fabricating the current time. Articles older than 48 hours are automatically flagged as "Published X days ago" to prevent stale news from posing as current breaking developments.

3. Speculative Claim Detection

Natural language pattern matching scans headlines and summaries for speculative language (e.g., "unconfirmed reports", "claims without evidence", "alleged", "sources claim", "rumors that"). Articles matching these indicators receive a distinct amber warning badge, ensuring readers recognize developing situations without mistaking unbacked assertions for established facts.

4. Breaking News Qualification Guards

Breaking news qualification requires three strict criteria: (1) an explicit breaking prefix from the publisher (e.g., BREAKING:, ALERT:, JUST IN:) or high-confidence emergency disaster verification, (2) verified publication within the last 6 hours, and (3) zero speculative claim flags. If no verified breaking stories exist, the ticker seamlessly presents Top Stories instead of fabricating alerts.

Source Reliability & Trust Tiers

Direct feeds from established institutions prioritized over algorithmic aggregators.

Tier 1: Primary News Publishers

Established public broadcasters and independent news organizations with dedicated editorial boards, rigorous fact-checking protocols, and original investigative reporting.

  • BBC News, DW News, NPR News: Public service broadcasting with statutory impartiality.
  • The Guardian, Al Jazeera, CNN: Global news networks with worldwide bureau reporting.
  • TechCrunch, Ars Technica, CNBC: Specialized reporting in technology, science, and markets.
Tier 2: Algorithmic Aggregators

Secondary syndication feeds that curate articles algorithmically from third-party publishers. NewsHub extracts the true underlying publisher for transparent attribution.

  • Google News: Automated crawler aggregating diverse regional reporting.
  • Attribution Extraction: Titles ending with - Publisher are automatically parsed to attribute original creators.
  • Priority Fallback: In deduplication disputes, Tier 1 direct feeds take precedence over Tier 2 aggregators.

System Capabilities, Boundaries & Limitations

Complete transparency regarding what automated aggregation can and cannot verify.

What NewsHub DOES Verify
  • Feed Origin Authenticity: Validates that feeds originate from official, publisher-owned endpoints.
  • Link Health & Security: Audits link syntax and normalizes canonical links, discarding malicious protocols.
  • Deduplication Integrity: Detects and merges cross-syndicated articles to prevent repetitive feeds.
  • Timestamp Accuracy: Preserves authentic publication dates, identifying stale stories older than 48 hours.
  • Speculative Language Detection: Flags headlines containing unverified assertions or rumors.
What NewsHub CANNOT Verify (Limitations)
  • Primary Investigative Truth: Automated software cannot conduct on-the-ground investigative reporting. We rely on the established editorial standards of Tier 1 publishers.
  • Real-World Retractions: If a publisher updates an article after publication without updating their RSS feed, there may be a cache delay of up to 5 minutes before changes reflect.
  • Paywalled Article Full-Text: In adherence with copyright law, NewsHub never scrapes full text behind subscription paywalls.
  • Zero Fabrication Policy: NewsHub will never fabricate news or label an unverified claim as confirmed without direct evidence from trusted primary feeds.
