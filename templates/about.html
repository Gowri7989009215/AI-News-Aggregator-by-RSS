Foundational Pillars

Built on Principle, Not Profit

Decentralized Feeds

Built entirely upon open RSS 2.0 and Atom specifications. We honor the open web by ingesting official publisher feeds directly without intermediate proprietary walled gardens.

Ethical Scraping

Polite User-Agent disclosure, strict rate-limiting, and OpenGraph metadata extraction. We never bypass paywalls or scrape full articles—every reader is directed back to the original source.

Technical Architecture

How NewsHub Works Under the Hood

From publisher publish-event to your screen in milliseconds: an end-to-end overview of our ingestion, sanitization, categorization, and rendering pipeline.

1
Feed Ingestion

Python ThreadPoolExecutor fires parallel requests across 10 official RSS endpoints (BBC, CNN, The Guardian, Al Jazeera, NPR, TechCrunch, Ars Technica, CNBC, DW, Google News).

ThreadPoolExecutor (8 workers)
2
XML/RSS Parsing

feedparser parses XML/RDF trees, extracts entry titles, permanent links, publication dates, and enclosed media objects (media:content, media:thumbnail).

feedparser 6.0
3
Sanitization & HTML Strip

BeautifulSoup4 scrubs CDATA, unwanted tracking scripts, broken HTML entities, and ad banners from article summaries, leaving pure, crisp text snippets.

BeautifulSoup4
4
Ethical OpenGraph Scraper

For entries missing thumbnails or deep previews, ScraperService politely extracts og:image, og:description, and author info on-demand with caching.

OpenGraph / Schema.org
5
Heuristic Classification

CategoryService applies weighted keyword matching and velocity algorithms to classify articles into topics and rank trending pulse stories.

Weighted Heuristics
6
Thread-Safe In-Memory Cache

Aggregated results are stored in an in-memory cache dictionary with a 5-minute TTL. Subsequent page views render instantly with zero network latency.

In-Memory TTL (5 min)

Ethical & Legal Web Scraping Policy

Our formal commitment to copyright compliance, publisher respect, and fair use.

1. Full Attribution & Deep-Linking

NewsHub does not republish full article bodies. Every single article card, headline, and preview prominently displays the original publisher's brand name, branding color, and provides direct outbound links to the publisher's official website.

2. Public Syndication Standards

All news feeds ingested are officially provided public RSS 2.0 endpoints intended by publishers for public syndication and distribution across readers and aggregators.

3. Transparent User-Agent Identification

Every HTTP request made by our engine identifies itself with a transparent User-Agent header: NewsHub-Aggregator/2.0 (+http://localhost:5000/about), allowing publishers to audit traffic origin and policies.

4. Respect for Robots.txt & Rate Limiting

Our scraper honors publisher access control instructions, maintains polite connection timeouts (4s max), implements in-memory caching to eliminate redundant requests, and never bypasses captchas or paywalls.
